Hi! My name is Chantal D'Amore

Postdoctoral Researcher in Social Psychology · University of Amsterdam

I am a Social Psychologist (PhD) and postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam, where I study the social-emotional dynamics of normative decline in collaboration with Gerben van Kleef.

My broader research interests lie at the intersection of political psychology and social psychology: polarization, moralization, norms, values, emotions, and social identities. I use experimental, longitudinal, and social interaction designs.

Here you can find more about my research, publications, and data & materials.

✉️ c.damore@uva.nl
